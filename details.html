About the Show

Life of Gautam Buddha is a powerful musical theatrical production created by Theatre World. Written and directed by renowned theatre personalities Lushin Dubey and Bubbles Sabharwal, the show brings to life the extraordinary journey of Prince Siddhartha — from a life of royal luxury to his quest for truth and final enlightenment as the Buddha.

Through a rich blend of drama, music and dance, the production explores the timeless themes of compassion, renunciation, suffering and inner peace. It remains deeply relevant in today’s world, offering audiences a moving experience of Buddha’s message of empathy, balance and equanimity.

The production has been performed across India (including Bodh Gaya) and internationally, and continues to inspire audiences with its powerful storytelling and artistic excellence.
